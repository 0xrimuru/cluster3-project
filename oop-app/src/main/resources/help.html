LPA Administration System - Help

The desktop interface of LPA eComms. It uses the same database and the same user accounts as the web app. What you see depends on your user group (Administrator or Staff). Customers use the web store.

Menu

  • Home - today's numbers: stock items, low / out of stock, invoices and total sales.
  • Stock Management - search stock by ID, name, brand or category. Staff can adjust stock (add, remove, reconcile) with a reason; every change is written to the audit history. Administrators can add, edit and delete items.

Sales and Invoicing

  • Invoices - search by invoice number, client, address or date (YYYY-MM-DD) and open an invoice. Administrators can delete an invoice; its items go back to stock.
  • New Invoice (Administrators) - type a Client ID and press Search Client, type a Stock ID and press Search Item, choose the quantity and press Add Item. Save Invoice saves it and reduces the stock. Prices always come from the catalogue.
  • Clients - search by name, ID, address or the exact phone number. Administrators can add, edit and disable clients.

Reports

  • Reorder planner - which items to buy first: out of stock and selling, under 30 days of stock, out of stock, low stock. Shows sales in the last 90 days, sales per day, days of stock left and a suggested order quantity. Export CSV opens in Excel.
  • Sales report - choose dates, Run report: sales per month (chart with a 3-month average) and the best-selling items. Export the invoices as CSV or save the chart as a picture.
  • Delivery route - the order to deliver the invoices of the chosen dates, starting and ending at the warehouse, with the total distance (approximate road kilometres).
  • Algorithm lab - times the sorting methods and shows the search-suggestion and cache statistics.

Tip: in New Invoice, start typing a client or item name (or ID) and pick it from the suggestions. An open invoice can be saved as a PDF with Save as PDF.

System Administration

  • My User Details - your account and Change password.
  • User Management (Administrators) - add, edit, deactivate and delete users.
  • Dark theme - switch between light and dark.

Exit

  • Logout - back to the sign-in window. Shutdown - close the program.

Security

After 5 wrong passwords the user name is locked for 15 minutes. Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes and client phone numbers are stored encrypted.
